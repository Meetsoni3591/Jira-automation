# Test Cases for T1.1 - Project Objectives

Okay, here are two detailed test cases based on the given "Project Objectives" task, aiming for different angles of user-friendliness in the online shopping platform.

**Test Case 1**

*   **Title:** Navigation - Product Search and Filtering Functionality

*   **Description:** Verify that the product search and filtering functionality allows users to easily find desired products with relevant and accurate results, enhancing user experience and product discovery. This tests a core aspect of user-friendliness for an online shopping platform.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  Navigate to the homepage of the online shopping platform.
    2.  In the search bar, enter a specific product name (e.g., "Red Leather Jacket").
    3.  Press the "Enter" key or click the "Search" button.
    4.  On the search results page, verify the following:
        *   All displayed products are related to the search query "Red Leather Jacket."
        *   The total number of search results is displayed.
        *   Sorting options (e.g., "Price: Low to High," "Newest Arrivals") are available and functional.
    5.  Use available filters (e.g., "Category," "Price Range," "Brand," "Size," "Color").
    6.  Apply a filter (e.g., filter by "Price Range: $50-$100").
    7.  Verify the following after applying the filter:
        *   The number of search results is updated accurately.
        *   All displayed products meet the specified filter criteria (e.g., all displayed products are within the $50-$100 price range and are related to "Red Leather Jacket").
    8.  Clear all applied filters.
    9.  Verify that all previously filtered products are displayed, and the filter is removed.
    10. Enter a broad search term(e.g. "shoes").
    11. Verify that products relevant to "shoes" are displayed, and the related category is listed.

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   Step 3: Search results display products accurately related to the search term.
    *   Step 4: All products shown relate to the query, the number of results displays correctly, and sorting functions work as expected.
    *   Step 7: The displayed products are filtered correctly based on the specified filter criteria. The number of search results corresponds to the filtered set.
    *   Step 9: The filter is cleared, and the original set of products is displayed.
    *   Step 11: Products of various shoe types are displayed with the related category, such as "sneakers" or "heels".

*   **Priority:** High (Essential for user experience; impacts product discoverability and conversion rates).

**Test Case 2**

*   **Title:** Checkout Process - Guest Checkout Functionality

*   **Description:** Verify the usability and functionality of the guest checkout process, ensuring a streamlined and frictionless purchase experience for users who choose not to create an account. This tests the platform's ability to cater to users who prioritize speed and ease of purchase.

*   **Steps:**

    1.  Browse the online shopping platform and add a product to the shopping cart.
    2.  Navigate to the shopping cart page.
    3.  Review the items in the cart and proceed to checkout.
    4.  On the checkout page, locate and select the "Guest Checkout" option (if available).
    5.  Enter the required billing information (e.g., Name, Address, Email, Phone Number).
    6.  Enter the required shipping information (if different from billing).
    7.  Select a shipping method (e.g., Standard, Express).
    8.  Select a payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal).
    9.  Enter the payment details (e.g., Credit Card Number, Expiration Date, CVV).
    10. Review the order summary, including billing, shipping, and payment information.
    11. Confirm the order.
    12. After the order is placed, verify the order confirmation page is displayed.
    13. Check the email inbox used during checkout for an order confirmation email.
    14. Attempt to place a duplicate order, but intentionally enter incorrect shipping or billing information.

*   **Expected Result:**

    *   Step 4: The user can smoothly and clearly locate and select the Guest Checkout option.
    *   Steps 5-9: The form fields for billing, shipping, and payment are clear, properly validated (e.g., for valid email format, credit card number format), and accept the correct type of input. Appropriate error messages are displayed for invalid input.
    *   Step 11: The order is successfully placed.
    *   Step 12: The user is redirected to a clear and informative order confirmation page with the order number and estimated delivery date.
    *   Step 13: The user receives an order confirmation email to the specified email address.
    *   Step 14: An informative error message is displayed indicating the incorrect information, and the order is not placed.

*   **Priority:** High (Impacts conversion rates and caters to a large segment of users who prefer guest checkout).
